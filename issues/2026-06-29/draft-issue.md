This is the first issue of London Seed Radar.

Every fortnight I send every newly funded pre-seed, seed, and Series A
company in the UK, London first: what they do, who founded them, roughly
how big the team is, and who is hiring right now, with roles a recent
grad could apply for flagged. It is all built from public sources,
Companies House filings, funding press, and public job boards, and every
company clears four checks before it makes the issue: it is in the UK, it
is early-stage, the round is recent, and the raise is corroborated by a
filing or a press source. When fewer than five qualify, I say so rather
than pad.

Five made the launch fortnight. Here they are.

This fortnight: 5 newly funded early-stage companies across the UK, London first.
