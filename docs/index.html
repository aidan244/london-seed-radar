London Seed Radar

A biweekly briefing on every newly funded pre-seed, seed, and Series A company in the UK, London first: what they do, who founded them, how big the team is, and who is hiring right now. Built on Companies House filings, funding press, and public ATS job boards. For founders, operators, and angels in the UK early-stage scene, and for students hunting internships and first jobs at startups.

The first issue is on its way. The archive, the live hiring list, and the public dataset all go live here with it.

Names, public roles, and public filings only; no personal contact data. The pipeline behind this is open source at github.com/aidan244/london-seed-radar. Corrections welcome via GitHub issues.
