Issue of 2026-08-10

3 newly funded early-stage companies across the UK this fortnight, London first.

LemonEdge (series-a, £15.8m, London)

LemonEdge builds fund accounting and operations software for private markets, replacing the spreadsheets and legacy systems that private equity firms, fund administrators, and family offices still run their accounting, data, and reporting on with one auditable platform.

Founders: David T. O'Malley (CEO and board chair (appointed around this round; not a founder)), Gareth Hewitt (Co-founder). Team: est. 26-50. Evidence: UKTN (lead).

Agon (seed, undisclosed, London)

Agon builds synthetic battlefields, virtual environments where autonomous defence systems such as counter-drone platforms train at machine speed against adaptive adversaries modelled on live conflict intelligence, long before they meet a real threat.

Founders: Junaid Hussain (Co-founder), Tristam Constant (Co-founder and CEO). Team: est. 11-25. Evidence: Tech.eu (lead).

Intropy (seed, undisclosed, London)

Intropy builds an AI-native operating system for the spare-parts trade: it plugs into a distributor's existing systems and automates the inventory, pricing, obsolescence, and salvage decisions that distributors, manufacturers, and recyclers still make by hand in spreadsheets.

Founders: Franziska Kirschner (Co-founder), Yih Kai Teh (Co-founder and CTO). Team: est. 11-25. Evidence: Tech.eu (lead).
