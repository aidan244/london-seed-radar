### 1. Callosum

Stage: seed (press label)
Amount: $100m (~£73-78m, figures vary by source and exchange rate)
Announced: 20 August 2026
Leg: websearch

What press says: Callosum, a London-based AI infrastructure startup founded
by Cambridge-trained neuroscientists Danyal Akarca and Jascha Achterberg,
has raised $100m in a seed round "to unify AI models and chips," breaking
AI workloads into subtasks and routing each to the best available chip or
model. The round was led by Atomico, with participation from Plural, DCVC,
and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company also received a $2.9m grant from
ARIA. EU-Startups describes it as "London-based" and one of the largest seed
rounds ever raised in Europe.

Sources:
- EU-Startups (primary, live URL confirmed via search): https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/08/london-based-callosum-raises-e85-4-million-in-atomico-led-seed-round-to-unify-ai-models-and-chips/

- Tech.eu: https://tech.eu/2026/08/20/callosum-raises-100m-seed-round/
- BusinessCloud: https://businesscloud.co.uk/news/ (search snippet only; direct URL not resolved)

FLAG for pipeline: $100m seed is exceptional in size, roughly 10-15x a
