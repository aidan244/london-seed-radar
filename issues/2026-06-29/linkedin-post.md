The UK graduate job market is cooked.

Graduate vacancies have fallen off a cliff. Entry-level postings are down
about a third since ChatGPT launched: for every graduate role out there,
they pull in 140 applications for that one spot on average.

So instead of sending application number 141, I pivoted. I started going
after roles at early-stage startups, not the oversubscribed grad schemes,
and I built something with Claude to find them.

It's a pipeline that finds every newly funded pre-seed, seed, and Series A
startup in the UK, London first, every two weeks. These are the companies
that just raised money and are about to start hiring, often before the
roles ever reach the big job boards.

Here is how it works:

1. WATCH. It scans public funding news and Companies House filings for
every new UK startup raise, with Claude agents sweeping the press for
anything the feeds miss.

2. SIEVE. Every company has to pass four filters: Is it in the UK? Is it
early-stage? Is the round recent? Is it real, backed by a filing or press,
not just a rumour? Miss one, you are out.

3. ENRICH. For the ones that pass, it pulls together what they do, who
founded them, roughly how big the team is (headcount and HQ checked through
Clay), and which roles they are hiring for right now, with the ones a
recent grad could actually apply for flagged.

4. PUBLISH. The handful that survive go out as one short briefing. This
fortnight, 5 made it out of dozens of raises: Conduct, Capsa AI, Isometric,
Houdini Bio, and Record OS. £91.4m between them. Public sources only,
nothing scraped, every claim linked back to its evidence.

I am going to keep doing this, 5 startups every two weeks, until I land a
job. (If you are hiring a Founder's Associate or an early-stage operator,
or you know someone who is, my DMs are open.)

If you want the list, or you are a student trying to find the startups that
are actually growing and hiring, subscribe. It's free.
