## 1 · Valarian 🟢 🎓

**Series A · £37.3m · London · sovereign infrastructure for defence and government AI**

Valarian builds sovereign infrastructure, delivered as a hardened Kubernetes control layer called ACRA, that lets governments, defence, and regulated enterprises run high-consequence AI and other sensitive workloads while keeping control over where their data lives and who can access it.

*Why it matters: the issue's biggest round, and a pointed one. While everyone else rushes AI into government and banking, Valarian is selling the controls: hard limits on where sensitive workloads run and who can switch them off. It is NEA's first defence and dual-use bet in Europe.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Maximilian (Max) Buchan (Co-founder and CEO; an early employee at digital asset manager CoinShares, where he helped launch one of the first publicly listed bitcoin exchange-traded products, before founding Valarian.), Joshua (Josh) McLaughlin (Co-founder and COO; a retired US Army officer and Ranger who previously led new market expansion at Palantir before co-founding Valarian.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 26-50 (Press reported about 31 staff in London around the Series A, 2026-07; consistent with the 26-50 bucket and the leadership team named on valarian.com/about. The 31 figure is press-reported and was not independently re-verified in this pass.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** 6 live role(s) on lever, including FinOps Associate 🎓, Paralegal, Platform Engineer
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12295525/filing-history) · [UKTN (lead)](https://www.uktech.news/ai/ai-infrastructure-firm-valarian-raises-37-3m-20260715)

## 2 · Arrakis 🟢 🎓

**Series A · $30m · London · AI agents for industry, aerospace to logistics**

Arrakis is an industrial AI company whose platform deploys AI agents into mission-critical operations for enterprises in sectors such as aerospace and defense, energy, engineering and construction, shipping, and telecommunications, without requiring them to replace their existing systems.

*Why it matters: out of stealth at seven months old, and aimed at the factory floor rather than the office: AI agents for aerospace, energy, and logistics, from ex-Accel and ex-Palantir founders betting the real AI money is industrial.*

## 3 · Meticulous 🟢 🎓

**Series A · $15m · London · AI that writes and maintains your app's UI tests**

Meticulous makes an AI testing tool that records real usage of a web app and automatically writes and maintains its end-to-end UI tests, catching visual and behavioural regressions so engineering teams do not have to hand-write frontend tests.

*Why it matters: nobody enjoys writing UI tests, so Meticulous stopped asking: it watches real usage and generates the suite. A brothers-built, YC-bred developer tool with a Palantir-Foundry pedigree, and it is hiring hard in London.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Gabriel Spencer-Harper (Co-founder and CEO; Co-founder and CEO of Meticulous, and its sole director on the Companies House register; previously an engineer at Dropbox and Opendoor.), Quentin Spencer-Harper (Co-founder and CTO; Co-founder and CTO of Meticulous and Gabriel's brother; spent about a decade leading frontend engineering on Palantir's Foundry product before starting the company.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (Bucketed estimate. The company's Y Combinator profile lists a team of roughly 15, and the live Ashby careers board shows 7 open roles (5 London, 2 San Francisco), 2026-07.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** 7 live role(s) on ashby, including Forward Deployed Engineer (New Grad) 🎓, Forward Deployed Engineer, London, Forward Deployed Engineer, San Francisco
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Software Testing Magazine (lead)](https://www.softwaretestingmagazine.com/news/meticulous-ai-automated-frontend-testing-platform-raises-15-million/)

## 4 · Tyred ⚪

**Seed · £2.5m · London · on-demand bike mechanics, and Lime's London pit crew**

Tyred is a London company that sends bike mechanics to riders on demand and services shared e-bike fleets, including roughly 50,000 of Lime's bikes in London, while building a connected 'ownership' platform that bundles bike repairs, maintenance, insurance, warranty, and finance.

*Why it matters: the consumer story in a B2B-heavy issue, and a refreshingly physical one. This is the crew who already keep about 50,000 of London's Lime bikes on the road, now building an ownership platform around them: unglamorous, and everywhere you look on a London street.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Lancelot Hoare (Co-founder and CEO; the company's sole Companies House-registered director, TYRED LTD 14293054, appointed on incorporation in August 2022.), Nikita Baranov (Co-founder and COO; named in the July 2026 seed-round press coverage as a co-founder of Tyred and its chief operating officer.), Daniel Bursztynski (Co-founder and CTO; named in the July 2026 seed-round press coverage as a co-founder of Tyred and its chief technology officer.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (No public headcount figure; qualitative estimate of the core team for a 2022-founded seed startup that has raised GBP2.5m and is hiring engineers. Its field mechanic operation is larger (100,000+ bikes serviced in the past year and a roughly 50,000-bike Lime fleet in London) and is likely partly a contractor network, so this bucket reflects the core company only and is uncertain (BikeBiz and ZAG Daily, 16 July 2026).)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board found
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [BikeBiz (lead)](https://bikebiz.com/tyred-raises-2-5-million-to-build-an-ai-powered-ownership-platform-for-cycling/)

## 5 · Prolo ⚪

**Seed · £4.2m · London · AI procurement for small construction firms**

Prolo is an AI-powered procurement platform for small and mid-sized construction firms, helping builders source, compare, and buy materials, equipment, and hire from a nationwide network of more than 185 supplier brands at competitive trade rates.

*Why it matters: AI pointed at the least-digitised corner of a huge industry, how small builders actually buy their materials. Its founder sold his last company to Matterport; this time he is after construction's procurement mess.*

- 👥 **Founders:** James Morris-Manuel (Founder and CEO; Previously founded Virtual Walkthrough, scaled it into a European market leader and led its acquisition by spatial data company Matterport, then served as EMEA Managing Director at Matterport, establishing its London regional headquarters; registered at Companies House as James David Morris-Manuel, a director since 25 June 2024.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (No headcount figure published in the funding press; the live public team page (https://prolo.io/team) lists 14 named team members, so bucketed as 11-25. Verified 2026-07-27.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board found
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [UKTN (lead)](https://www.uktech.news/ai/prolo-raises-4-2m-to-boost-procurement-for-sme-construction-firms-20260714)
