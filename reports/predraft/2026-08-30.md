### 1. Embedd (London, pre-seed, ~$2.7 million)

**Leg:** websearch
**Stage:** pre-seed
**Amount:** $2.7 million (EU-Startups quoted as EUR 2.3 million; no GBP figure in press)

**What the press says:** Embedd builds software infrastructure for physical AI, helping
