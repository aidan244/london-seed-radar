## 1 · Agon 🎓

**Seed · $30m · London · synthetic battlefields to train defensive AI**

Agon builds infrastructure for designing, training, and iterating AI systems for autonomous defence, running them against synthetic battlefields, virtual environments where a counter-drone platform can face a drone swarm thousands of times over before it ever meets a real one.

*Why it matters: one of the largest European defence seeds of the year, and a bet that the hard problem in autonomous defence is not the weapon, it is the training ground. Founded by an ex-Anduril director and chaired by Unity's founder, it is London defence-tech with serious pedigree behind it.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Tristam Constant (Co-founder and CEO; a former British Army officer and, per press, an ex-senior director at defence company Anduril; registered at Companies House as Tristam Charles Ashley Meyricke Constant.), Junaid Hussain (Co-founder; press reports him as a co-founder of Cambridge Aerospace and Iconic.). Chairman: David Helgason (founder and former CEO of Unity Technologies).
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (No published headcount; qualitative estimate for a defence startup fresh out of stealth with a $30m seed and offices in London and Berlin. Treat as uncertain and confirm before relying on it.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board confirmed in this pass (defence startups of this size usually hire fast; worth a manual check of agon.* careers before sending)
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Tech.eu (lead)](https://tech.eu/2026/07/29/british-defence-startup-agon-creating-virtual-battlefields-launches-raising-30m/) · [Army Technology](https://www.army-technology.com/news/agon-seed-round-funding/)

## 2 · LemonEdge

**Series A · £15.8m · London · fund accounting and operations for private markets**

LemonEdge builds fund accounting and operations software for private markets, automating the accounting, data management, and reporting that private equity firms, fund administrators, and large family offices still run across spreadsheets and legacy systems, inside one governed, auditable platform.

*Why it matters: the plumbing bet in this issue. Private markets have ballooned while the back office stayed manual; LemonEdge is selling the system of record, and Blackstone leading the round is a pointed vote from exactly the kind of firm that would use it.*

- 👥 **Founders:** David T. O'Malley (CEO; named in the funding coverage as appointed CEO during this round). The founding team is not named in the launch coverage; confirm on lemonedge.com before featuring names.
- 📊 **Team:** est. 26-50 (Bucketed estimate for a 2020-era fintech raising a $21m Series A and expanding across the US and Europe; no exact figure published in the funding press. Uncertain; confirm.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board confirmed in this pass

## 3 · Intropy

**Seed · $11m · London · an AI operating system for the spare-parts trade**

Intropy builds an AI-native operating system for the spare-parts distribution industry, plugging into a customer's existing ERP to automate the inventory, pricing, and day-to-day decisions that distributors, manufacturers, and recyclers still make by hand in spreadsheets.

*Why it matters: a deeply unglamorous, deeply large market, and exactly the kind of place AI can earn its keep quietly. Built by two ex-Tractable researchers who have done applied AI on messy industrial data before, backed by Felix Capital.*

- 👥 **Founders:** YihKai Teh (Co-founder and CTO; press reports him as a former researcher at UK AI company Tractable.), Franziska Kirschner (Co-founder; press reports her as a former researcher at Tractable.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (Bucketed estimate for a 2023-incorporated seed startup, INTROPY AI LTD; no exact headcount in the funding press. Confirm before relying on it.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board confirmed in this pass
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Tech.eu (lead)](https://tech.eu/2026/07/30/ai-for-spare-parts-startup-intropy-raises-11m/) · [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/07/london-based-intropy-raises-e9-5-million-to-build-the-ai-native-operating-system-for-the-spare-parts-industry) · Companies House: INTROPY AI LTD (15342920)

## 4 · Paddington Robotics

**Pre-seed · £1.9m (2025) · London · embodied AI, robots for the supermarket shop floor**

Paddington Robotics is building robots for human spaces, starting on the supermarket shop floor, to take the long, physically punishing tasks off staff, and to solve what its backers call the real-world data problem for embodied AI.

*Why it matters: not this fortnight's money, but a London robotics team worth knowing. Founder Zehan Wang sold his last company, Magic Pony, to Twitter; now he is chasing embodied AI in the messiest possible environment, the aisles of a working supermarket. One to watch for the next round.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Zehan Wang (Founder and CEO; the sole director of PADDINGTON ROBOTICS LIMITED at Companies House, appointed on incorporation 14 October 2024; previously co-founded Magic Pony Technology, acquired by Twitter in 2016. Press also names Johannes Totz and Yuntian Fang as co-founders; confirm before printing those names.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 1-10 (Qualitative estimate for an early London robotics company; no published headcount. Uncertain.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board confirmed (their site was pre-launch at last check; worth a look before sending)
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [The Bae HQ (£1.9m, 11 Feb 2025)](https://www.thebaehq.com/news/paddington-robotics-raises-1-9m-ai-robotics-7percent) · [7percent Ventures portfolio](https://www.7pc.vc/portfolio/paddington-robotics) · Companies House: PADDINGTON ROBOTICS LIMITED (16015385, London W10)

## 5 · Darcy 🟢 🎓

**No disclosed round · London · an AI agent that plans your event**

Darcy is an AI agent for event planning: it plans the event, books the vendors, manages the guests, and works the budget, aimed at the people who run events for a living rather than at one-off consumers.

*Why it matters: the hiring story in this issue. There is no disclosed round, but Darcy is a Project Europe-backed London startup with real alpha clients ahead of a September beta, and it is filling founding seats right now.
