**Callosum** (London, EC1/EC2 registered address not confirmed; London HQ
corroborated by EU-Startups, Tech.eu, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund press
release). AI orchestration software that routes workloads across AI models
and chip types. Founded 2025 by Danyal Akarca and Jascha Achterberg,
Cambridge-trained neuroscientists. Round led by Atomico, with Plural, DCVC,
and the UK Sovereign AI Fund (the Fund's first disclosed investment).
Supplementary ARIA grant of $2.9M for heterogeneous computing research.
The round is one of the largest seed rounds ever raised by a UK startup.
Secondary source: https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/08/london-based-callosum-raises-e85-4-million-in-atomico-led-seed-round-to-unify-ai-models-and-chips/

**Mindgard** (London registered: 34 Lime Street, EC3M 7AT, per Companies
House record 14120558; operational dual HQ London and Boston). AI security
platform: shadow AI discovery, AI red teaming, and runtime protection for
models, agents, and applications. Lancaster University spinout. Round led by
Album VC, with Karma Ventures, .406 Ventures, Atlantic Bridge, IQ Capital,
and Lakestar.
Secondary source: https://businesscloud.co.uk/news/lancaster-uni-spinout-mindgard-raises-another-22m/

**Metal Morph** (London-based per Tech.eu and The Chemical Engineer).
Recovers aluminium- and iron-based treatment chemicals from municipal and
industrial wastewater, returning them to water-treatment operations. Founded
2025 by Moletsane Mophethe. Round co-led by Sustainable Ventures and Green
Angel Ventures, with Aqcelerator and Found Capital; plus a separate £100k
Ofwat grant. Nine-person team; six UK water-utility partnerships covering
56% of the market.
Secondary source: https://www.uktech.news/climate-tech/metal-morph-raises-pre-seed-round-to-turn-wastewater-into-supply-chains-20260813

**FlairMakers** (UK geography passes; precise HQ not confirmed London).
Hospitality freelancer platform connecting venues with vetted professionals;
live in the UK and Romania. Founded by Raluca Epureanu and Alexis Sikorsky
(met at Oxford Said Business School). Round led by SFC Capital with angel
investors. Local pipeline should confirm the registered office to resolve
London vs rest-of-UK grouping.

**Cytix** (Manchester, confirmed by Crunchbase, StarupHub.ai, and Tracxn;
also corroborated by lead investor Northern Gritstone, which funds North of
England science and technology companies). Change risk management platform
for enterprises managing security risk from AI-driven software development.
Founded 2022 by Ben Armstrong and Thomas Ballin. Round led by Northern
Gritstone with Auriga Cyber Ventures and NPIF II (PXN Equity Finance).
