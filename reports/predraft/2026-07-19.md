### 1. Valarian

**Stage:** Series A
**Amount:** $50m (approx. £37.3m)
**Date:** 15 July 2026
**Leg:** websearch

Valarian is building what it describes as a sovereign infrastructure layer for
high-consequence operations and AI-driven systems, letting enterprises and
government departments control how their critical applications, AI models and
operational workloads communicate and access data, while enforcing governance at
the infrastructure level rather than the application level.

The round was led by New Enterprise Associates (NEA, described as its first
defence and dual-use investment in Europe) with participation from Lightbank,
XTX Markets, Sequel and LitVC, plus angels Gokul Rajaram and Nikesh Arora.
The company was founded by Max Buchan and Josh McLaughlin, both previously at
Palantir. The round brings total funding to $70m.

### 2. Applied Computing

**Stage:** Series A
**Amount:** $20m (approx. £15m)
**Date:** 15-16 July 2026
**Leg:** websearch

Applied Computing (formerly in stealth, now public at appliedcomputing.com)
builds Orbital, described as a foundation AI model for energy operators. The
platform combines physics-grounded AI with models for chemical engineering,
time-series forecasting and language, and is said to capture 100% of available
data from downstream energy facilities against an industry norm of 8%.

The round was led by KBR, the engineering and technology firm, with Databricks
Ventures also participating. The company was founded in 2023 by Callum Adamson
and Dr Sam Tukra; TechFundingNews describes it as an Imperial College spinout.
Applied Computing is headquartered in London and has opened a Houston office.

Sources:

### 3. Meticulous

**Stage:** Series A
**Amount:** $15m (approx. £11m)
**Date:** 15 July 2026
**Leg:** websearch

Meticulous builds an AI-powered automated software testing platform that records
application interactions during development and uses that data to generate and
maintain end-to-end test suites, without engineers writing the tests manually.
Customers cited in press coverage include Dropbox, Notion, Wiz and ElevenLabs.
The company reports 5x ARR growth year on year.

The round was led by Chemistry, a San Francisco-based VC, with participation
from Menlo Ventures. Angels included people from Poolside, Dropbox, Vercel,
OpenAI, Stripe, Adobe, Hex and Cursor. The company was founded by brothers
Gabriel Spencer-Harper and Quentin Spencer-Harper, previously at Palantir and
Dropbox respectively. Meticulous is London-based and YC-backed.

Sources:
- https://www.meticulous.ai/blog/series-a
- https://www.startupmag.co.uk/funding/uk-startup-funding-report-17-07-2026/

### 4. Tyred

**Stage:** Seed
**Amount:** £2.5m
**Date:** 16 July 2026
**Leg:** websearch

Tyred is a London-based startup that provides an AI-powered ownership platform
for cyclists, bundling predictive maintenance (via IoT devices embedded in bikes),
repairs, insurance, warranty management and finance into one digital product.
The company has serviced more than 100,000 bikes and provides maintenance and
battery-swapping for Lime's London fleet of around 50,000 bikes.

The round was led by Raw Ventures and Ada Ventures, with angel participation
from Anton Buzdalin. Tyred was founded in 2022 by Lancelot Hoare, Nikita Baranov
and Daniel Bursztynski.

### 5. BeCertain

**Stage:** Pre-seed
**Amount:** £1.7m
**Date:** 16 July 2026
**Leg:** websearch

BeCertain is an AI software assistant for dental clinicians that interprets 2D
intraoral X-rays, detecting conditions including tooth decay, bone loss, infections
and restoration defects. Its described differentiator is uncertainty-aware AI:
rather than a binary verdict, it produces calibrated confidence scores alongside
each finding so a clinician reviewing an ambiguous image can see how much weight
the model places on its assessment.

The round was led by Sure Valley Ventures (SVV), with support from Innovate
UK's Investor Partnership Growth Catalyst programme. BeCertain is a joint
spinout from King's College London and the University of Surrey; the CEO and
co-founder is Dr Yunpeng Li, Reader in AI and Digital Oral Health at the KCL
Faculty of Dentistry, and the CMO and co-founder is Professor Owen Addison.

Note: the company is a spinout from both KCL (London) and the University of
Surrey (Guildford). Verify that the registered address is London and that
operational substance is London-based before featuring as a London company.
An Investegate RNS from Mindflair PLC (July 16) records the investment
announcement.

Sources:

- https://www.surrey.ac.uk/news/surrey-and-kings-ai-spinout-raises-ps17m-tackle-above-50-cent-dental-diagnostic-error-rates
