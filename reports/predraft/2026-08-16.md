### 2. Metal Morph

**Stage:** Pre-seed
**Amount:** GBP 700,000 equity (+ GBP 100,000 Ofwat innovation grant)
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Geography:** London (confirmed by multiple sources including UKTN and Tech.eu)
**Leg:** websearch

According to press coverage, Metal Morph is a London-based cleantech startup that
"develops an advanced chemical recovery platform that integrates directly into
existing municipal and industrial wastewater treatment plants," recovering
aluminium- and iron-based treatment chemicals for reuse. Founded by Moletsane
Mophethe, who "experienced water scarcity firsthand during his childhood in
Lesotho," the round was co-led by Sustainable Ventures and Green Angel Ventures,
with Aqcelerator and Found Capital also participating. The company has partnerships
with "six UK water utilities, including Anglian Water and Thames Water."
