## Company

- One-liner: Meticulous makes an AI testing tool that records real usage of a web app and automatically writes and maintains its end-to-end UI tests, catching visual and behavioural regressions so engineering teams do not have to hand-write frontend tests. (source: https://www.meticulous.ai (verified 2026-07-27))
- Website: https://www.meticulous.ai
- Companies House number: 14177185
- Incorporated: 2022-06-16
- Registered office: 5 New Street Square, London, EC4A 3TW
- Headcount estimate: 11-25 (source: Bucketed estimate. The company's Y Combinator profile lists a team of roughly 15, and the live Ashby careers board shows 7 open roles (5 London, 2 San Francisco), 2026-07.)
