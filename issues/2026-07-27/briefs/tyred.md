## Company

- One-liner: Tyred is a London company that sends bike mechanics to riders on demand and services shared e-bike fleets, including roughly 50,000 of Lime's bikes in London, while building a connected 'ownership' platform that bundles bike repairs, maintenance, insurance, warranty, and finance. (source: https://tyred.io (on-demand mobile mechanic service) and https://bikebiz.com/tyred-raises-2-5-million-to-build-an-ai-powered-ownership-platform-for-cycling/ (Lime fleet and ownership platform); both verified 2026-07-27)
- Website: https://tyred.io
- Companies House number: 14293054
- Incorporated: 2022-08-12
- Registered office: 5th Floor Palladium House, 1-4 Argyll Street, London, W1F 7TA
- Headcount estimate: 11-25 (source: No public headcount figure; qualitative estimate of the core team for a 2022-founded seed startup that has raised GBP2.5m and is hiring engineers. Its field mechanic operation is larger (100,000+ bikes serviced in the past year and a roughly 50,000-bike Lime fleet in London) and is likely partly a contractor network, so this bucket reflects the core company only and is uncertain (BikeBiz and ZAG Daily, 16 July 2026).)
