## 1 · geoSurge ⚪

**Seed · £9.4m · London · how your brand shows up inside AI search and chatbots**

geoSurge is a London deep-tech company whose platform helps brands measure, understand, and improve how they are represented inside large language models and AI search such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, using its proprietary Corpus Engineering method.

*Why it matters: a bet that "how do we show up in ChatGPT" becomes a budget line, and it is the largest seed yet in a category, GEO, that barely had a name a year ago.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Francisco Vigo (Co-founder and CEO; from Argentina, an economist and data scientist focused on large-scale data systems and machine learning; developed geoSurge's Corpus Engineering method), Jons Mostovojs (Co-founder and CTO; an AI and machine learning engineer who has previously served as a CTO and chief architect)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (soft estimate; no public figure, press says headcount doubled since a 2025 stealth exit)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no individual roles listed; their [careers page](https://geosurge.ai/careers) says they are hiring across engineering, research, product, growth, and operations
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/16249592/filing-history) · [Startups Magazine](https://startupsmagazine.co.uk/geosurge-raises-12m-behind-a-new-thesis-for-ai-visibility) · [UKTN (lead)](https://www.uktech.news/ai/ai-search-visibility-startup-geosurge-scores-9-4m-round-20260703)

## 2 · StirlingX ⚪

**Series A · £15m · London · sovereign data platform for defence and infrastructure**

StirlingX runs a sovereign data intelligence platform that captures, secures, and fuses multi-sensor data into defence-grade insight for critical national infrastructure, defence, and government customers.

*Why it matters: the biggest round in the issue, defence-grade data infrastructure spun out of the GALLOS studio, with a former GCHQ director in the chair.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Dean Jones (Founder and CEO; co-founder of GALLOS Technologies, the venture studio that incubated StirlingX; former Royal Marines and a Sloan Fellow at London Business School), Joshua Burch (Co-founder and Director; co-founder of GALLOS, former Head of Strategy and Risk at GCHQ, appointed to the UK National Cyber Advisory Board in 2025)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (estimate from the team page and the Series A plan to grow engineering)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board found (careers routed informally; any roles are LinkedIn-only, not counted)
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/14111820/filing-history) · [UKTN (lead)](https://www.uktech.news/defence-tech/stirlingx-raises-15m-to-scale-sovereign-data-intelligence-platform-20260702)

## 3 · Kord 🔒

**Series A · £6.4m · London · identity, compliance, and payments for regulated industries**

Kord is an FCA-regulated platform that combines identity verification, AML and compliance checks, client onboarding and e-signing, and payment processing into a single system for regulated industries such as estate agents, law firms, and conveyancers.

*Why it matters: unsexy, sticky fintech, identity checks, compliance, and payments welded into one system for the estate agents, law firms, and conveyancers still stitching it together by hand.*

- 👥 **Founders:** James Owusu (Founder and CEO; founded the company as Checkboard in 2019, after a slow and costly personal property transaction; its B2B arm now trades as Kord)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 26-50 (estimate from Series A stage, revenue growing to about £4m, and 100+ customers; no public figure)
- 📈 **Hiring:** 🔒 unverified; kord.com blocks automated checks, so open roles could not be confirmed. Worth a look at kord.com/careers in a browser
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [UKTN (lead)](https://www.uktech.news/fintech/kord-secures-6-4m-to-process-payments-for-regulated-industries-20260708)

## 4 · Whering 🟢 🌐

**Seed · £5.2m · London · digital wardrobe app, ten million users**

Whering is a digital wardrobe app that lets people catalogue the clothes they already own, plan and style outfits from them, and shop more intentionally.

*Why it matters: the rare consumer story in a B2B-heavy issue, ten million people cataloguing what they already own, and it is hiring across six roles, four of them remote.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Bianca Rangecroft (Founder and CEO; a director since incorporation in 2020, she started Whering to solve her own wardrobe-management problem and has led it to 10m users)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 11-25 (the about page describes a team of over 20, based in Farringdon)
- 📈 **Hiring:** 🟢 6 live roles on their [careers page](https://whering.co/careers), 4 remote-friendly, from Senior Product Engineer to Comms and Content roles
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Tech.eu (lead)](https://tech.eu/2026/07/07/whering-lands-7m-as-digital-wardrobe-platform-reaches-10m-users/)

## 5 · Xylo ⚪

**Pre-seed · £2.8m · London · AI for the paperwork behind planning applications**

Xylo builds an AI-agent workspace for UK local-authority planning teams that automates the administrative work on planning applications, chasing documents, verifying submissions, and compiling responses, while final judgement stays with planning officers.

*Why it matters: aiming AI agents at the administrative drag in Britain's planning system while leaving the judgement to officers; early, but the bottleneck is real and everywhere.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Dermot O'Riordan (Co-founder; a former lawyer and operator with experience at Hogan Lovells and Orrick), Habs Kim (Co-founder; an engineering co-founder, previously at Cytora and Marshmallow)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 1-10 (pre-seed, two co-founder directors, no team page yet)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board found (any roles are LinkedIn-only, not counted)
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [Investegate (Mindflair RNS)](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/mindflair--mfai/uk-govtech-ai-business-xylo-raises-2-8-million/9647620)
