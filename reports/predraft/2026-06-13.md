## This week's candidates (WebSearch leg)

### 1. Geordie AI

**Stage/amount:** Series A, $30M  
**Announced:** 28 May 2026  
**Leg:** websearch  

According to Fortune and Balderton Capital's own announcement, Geordie AI is a London-based startup building a security and governance layer for enterprise AI agents: it monitors, audits, and enforces policy on AI agents operating inside corporate infrastructure. The $30M Series A was led by Balderton Capital, with co-investors Crosspoint Capital, General Catalyst, and Ten Eleven. Post-money valuation was approximately $180M, described by Balderton as the largest Series A for a cybersecurity startup in Europe to date.

### 2. Gradient Labs

**Stage/amount:** Series A extension, total round $26M (fresh tranche $13M)  
**Announced:** 1-3 June 2026  
**Leg:** websearch  

Sifted and Tech.eu report that Gradient Labs, a London startup founded by former members of the Monzo AI and data science team, doubled its Series A from $13M to $26M with a fresh $13M led by Octopus Ventures and CommerzVentures, with follow-on from Redpoint Ventures and Exceptional Capital. The company builds AI agents that automate regulated financial tasks (customer support, KYC, disputes, lending workflows) for financial services firms. Named customers include Wise, Monzo, and Zego.

Note: this is an extension of an existing Series A, not a fresh first close. The pipeline's dedup logic should check whether the original close was already ingested.

### 3. Lune & Wild

**Stage/amount:** Series A, £2M (approximately €2.3M)  
**Announced:** 1 June 2026  
**Leg:** websearch  

Multiple sources (EU-Startups, FoodBev Media, startup.eu) report that Lune & Wild, a London-based children's food brand, raised £2M in a Series A led by Guinness Ventures. The company was founded by cousins Lara Rodgers and Nadia Simonds in 2021 from frustration at the low nutritional quality and flavour of mainstream baby food. Investors also include Dr. Rupy Aujla and Doug Struthers (ex-managing director of Ella's Kitchen). The funds are earmarked for production capacity, retail expansion (including a recent Ocado listing), marketing, and staff hires.

Sources:

### 4. Poindexter Labs

**Stage/amount:** Seed, £2M  
**Announced:** 2 June 2026  
**Leg:** websearch  

Tech.eu, pulse2.com, and TFN report that Poindexter Labs, a London startup founded in 2025 by Jocelyn D'Arcy (a former mathematics teacher and ex-chief of staff at the National Mathematics and Science College), raised an oversubscribed £2M seed co-led by Episode 1 and First Cheque, with participation from Venture Friends partners. The company provides expert-generated training data for frontier AI models, connecting frontier labs with domain specialists (mathematicians, scientists) who annotate data at a high level of rigour. The round was described as oversubscribed; some contributors (the annotators themselves) invested personal savings.

### Scope

**Stage/amount:** Series A, €17.3M ($20M)  
**Announced:** 22 May 2026 (22 days before 13 June; window opens 23 May)  
**Leg:** websearch  

EU-Startups, Sifted, and BeBeez all date the announcement to 22 May 2026, which is one day outside the 21-day lookback window. Scope is a London startup founded in 2024 that builds AI workflow software for the testing, inspection, and certification (TIC) industry.
