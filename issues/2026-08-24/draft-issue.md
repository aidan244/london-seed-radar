# London

## 1 · Paralo ⚪

**Pre-seed · £270k · London · one operating system for a golf club, tee sheet to till**

Paralo builds a single operating system for golf clubs, replacing the separate legacy tools a club runs its tee sheet, competitions, live scoring, member app, till, and finances on, plus a connected app for the golfers themselves.

*Why it matters: the smallest round in this issue and the clearest customer. A golf club runs its tee sheet, competitions, scoring, member app, till and books on separate legacy systems, and Paralo is trying to be all of them at once. Founder-led and oversubscribed, with SEIS angels rather than an institutional lead, which tells you the founders backed themselves first.*

## 2 · Medly AI ⚪

**Seed · $8m · London · an AI tutor for GCSE, A-Level, IB and SAT revision**

Medly builds an AI tutor for exam revision, giving students personalised practice and instant feedback across GCSE, A-Level, IB, AP, and SAT courses.

*Why it matters: 400,000 UK users since launching in February 2025, built by two UCL medicine graduates who went into revision instead of medicine. Felix Capital leading is the interesting part: a consumer investor betting that AI tutoring sells to parents and students directly, not to schools.*

## 3 · Metal Morph ⚪

**Pre-seed · £700k · London · recovering treatment chemicals from wastewater**

Metal Morph recovers the aluminium and iron-based treatment chemicals that water and wastewater plants would otherwise discharge, and puts them back into the treatment process on site, so a plant buys less new chemical and sends less waste away.

*Why it matters: the least glamorous company in this issue and possibly the most useful. Water companies buy aluminium and iron coagulants, use them once, and send them down the line; Metal Morph pulls them back out on site and returns them. Six UK utility partnerships, reportedly including Thames Water and Anglian Water, before a £700k pre-seed is unusual traction for a company incorporated in February 2025.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Moletsane Mophethe (Founder; The sole director and sole person with significant control of METAL MORPH LTD since its incorporation in February 2025.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 1-10 (Scout reporting described a nine-person team in August 2026, consistent with a company incorporated in February 2025 with one director on the register. Press-reported, not independently verified.)

## 4 · Mindgard ⚪

**Series A · £22.2m · London · finds, red teams and defends a company's AI systems**

Mindgard is an AI security platform that finds the AI systems an organisation is actually running, including the ones nobody registered, red teams them for weaknesses, and then defends the models, agents, and applications at runtime.

*Why it matters: the largest disclosed round this fortnight, and a reminder of how long a university spinout actually takes: ten years of Lancaster research, four years as a company, and now $30m to go after enterprises that are only now discovering how much AI their own staff are running unsupervised. Note the dual London and Boston headquarters; this is not a purely London company.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Peter Garraghan (Founder and Chief Science Officer; A Lancaster University professor whose AI security research the company was spun out of. A director of MINDGARD LTD from its incorporation in May 2022.)
- 📊 **Team:** est. 26-50 (Bucketed estimate, not a published figure: no headcount appears on the site or in the funding coverage. Inferred from a six-person named leadership team, dual Boston and London operations, and roughly $42m raised in total. Uncertain; confirm before relying on it.)
- 📈 **Hiring:** ⚪ no public job board found
- 🧾 **Evidence:** [UKTN (lead)](https://www.uktech.news/ai/lancaster-spinout-mindgard-raises-22m-to-scale-ai-security-platform-20260813)

## 5 · Cytix 🟢

**Series A · $7m · Manchester · continuous security review for software that never stops changing**

Cytix is a security decision layer for teams whose software never stops changing: it assesses the risk each change introduces, continuously rather than at an annual pen test, and automates the approvals that follow.

*Why it matters: the first company this briefing has featured from outside London, and it earned the slot: a $7m Series A led by Northern Gritstone, four roles open right now, and a product aimed squarely at the gap between how fast teams ship software in 2026 and how slowly security review happens.*

- 👥 **Founders:** Ben Armstrong (Co-founder and CEO; Named as chief executive in the Series A coverage. Registered at Companies House as Benjamin John Armstrong, a director of CYTIX LTD since October 2022.), Thomas Ballin (Co-founder; Registered at Companies House as Thomas Jacob Ballin, a director of CYTIX LTD from its incorporation in April 2022.)
