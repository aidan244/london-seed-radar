Issue of 2026-08-24

5 newly funded early-stage companies across the UK this fortnight, London first.

Mindgard (series-a, £22.2m, London)

Mindgard is an AI security platform that finds the AI systems an organisation is actually running, including the ones nobody registered, red teams them for weaknesses, and then defends the models, agents, and applications at runtime.

Founders: Peter Garraghan (Founder and Chief Science Officer). Team: est. 26-50. Evidence: UKTN (lead).

Metal Morph (pre-seed, £700k, London)

Metal Morph recovers the aluminium and iron-based treatment chemicals that water and wastewater plants would otherwise discharge, and puts them back into the treatment process on site, so a plant buys less new chemical and sends less waste away.

Founders: Moletsane Mophethe (Founder). Team: est. 1-10. Evidence: Startups Magazine, UKTN (lead).

Paralo (pre-seed, £270k, London)

Paralo builds a single operating system for golf clubs, replacing the separate legacy tools a club runs its tee sheet, competitions, live scoring, member app, till, and finances on, plus a connected app for the golfers themselves.

Founders: Jarrad Hicks (Co-founder and CEO), Zaheer Merali (Co-founder and CTO). Team: est. 1-10. Evidence: Companies House, Tech.eu.

Cytix (series-a, undisclosed, UK)

Cytix is a security decision layer for teams whose software never stops changing: it assesses the risk each change introduces, continuously rather than at an annual pen test, and automates the approvals that follow.

Founders: Ben Armstrong (Co-founder and CEO), Thomas Ballin (Co-founder). Team: est. 11-25. Hiring: 4 live role(s), site. Evidence: Companies House, Tech.eu (lead).

Medly AI (seed, undisclosed, London)

Medly builds an AI tutor for exam revision, giving students personalised practice and instant feedback across GCSE, A-Level, IB, AP, and SAT courses.

Founders: Kavi Samra (Co-founder), Paul Jung (Co-founder and CEO). Team: est. 11-25. Evidence: Tech.eu.
