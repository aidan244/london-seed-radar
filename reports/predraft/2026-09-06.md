### 1. Embedd (pre-seed, £2m / ~$2.7m)

**Announced:** 2026-08-24
**Location:** London (confirmed by EU-Startups: "London-based Embedd")
**Stage:** Pre-seed
**Amount:** £2m (reported variously as $2.7m / €2.3m by different outlets)
**Investors:** Seedcamp (lead), Cocoa, Connect Ventures, 2100 Ventures, Vesna Capital,

### 3. AI Score (seed, £4m / ~$5.4m / ~E4.6m)

**Announced:** 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-04 (two outlets dated the same round on
consecutive days)
**Location:** London (confirmed by EU-Startups headline: "London's AI Score")
**Stage:** Seed (follows a $1m / €864k pre-seed in November 2025)
**Amount:** £4m (reported as $5.4m / €4.6m; Startupmag and Resultsense use the GBP figure)
**Investors:** Fuel Ventures (lead), GALLOS Technologies (continuing); angels include
Alan Morgan (co-founder, MMC Ventures), Mo El Husseiny (Ventura Capital), Robert Mann
(Marshall Bridge Ventures)
**What they do (press-only):** AI Score builds an enterprise AI governance platform.
It sits between an organisation's AI policies and the systems, employees, agents, and
workflows using AI in practice, continuously collecting and analysing AI activity to
provide a live view of the company's AI landscape. Customers include law firms, FTSE
250 companies, a UK fintech, and global consumer brands per the press.
**Founders:** Alex Harland and Benita Tibb (named in Vestbee and Konsulteer coverage).
The company has a national security advisory background: UKTN's earlier November 2025
article described it as emerging from stealth with national security advisers on board.
