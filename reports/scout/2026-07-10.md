### Company notes

**geoSurge** (London): AI visibility startup helping brands shape how they appear inside generative AI systems (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude). Founded 2025. Method called Corpus Engineering targets training-data influence rather than search-style citation tracking. Round led by AlbionVC; also includes Play Ventures, Octopus Ventures, Celero Ventures, Boost Capital, and angels from Google DeepMind and Microsoft AI. Corroborated by: EU-Startups, Startups Magazine, Retail Technology Innovation Hub.

**StirlingX** (London, Companies House 14111820, 86-90 Paul Street EC2A 4NE): Sovereign intelligence and autonomy platform for defence and critical national infrastructure. Emerged from incubation inside GALLOS Technologies on 2026-07-02. Former GCHQ Director Sir Jeremy Fleming appointed chairman. Series A led by Ventura Capital and RCM Private Markets Master Fund (Rokos Capital Management US). Prior $11m seed raised earlier in 2026. Total funding $31m. Corroborated by: Tech.eu, BusinessCloud, SiliconAngle. Note: some sources describe operations sites in Cambridge and Oxford; Companies House registration and multiple press sources confirm London HQ.

**Axle Energy** (London): Virtual power plant (VPP) software aggregating home energy assets (EV chargers, home batteries, heat pumps) into electricity markets. Founded 2023 by former Bulb executive Karl Bach and data scientist Archy de Berker. Over 300,000 connected devices, 2GW of flexible capacity. Series A led by Energize Capital; also includes Accel, Picus Capital, and Eka Ventures. Corroborated by: EU-Startups, finsmes.com, Energize Capital blog.

**Kord** (London): FCA-regulated fintech unifying identity verification, onboarding, and payment processing for regulated industries: estate agents, law firms, and financial services. CEO James Owusu. Revenue up 7x, 100+ customers, £5.4m processed in the past year. Series A led by Guinness Ventures; also Beringea and SFC Capital. Total funding £9m. Corroborated by: Tech.eu, Fintech Global, Finextra.

**Stoa** (London): Consumer fintech offering upfront lifestyle rewards on fixed-term cash deposits (product: "Stoa Pots"). Connects savers, regulated banking partners, and merchant brands. FSCS-covered deposits. CEO Mike Saraswat. Pre-seed co-led by Bespokeist Partners and Ingenii Capital; also Force Over Mass Capital and Fuel Ventures. Corroborated by: Tech.eu, EU-Startups, Fintech Global.

**Neuronostics** (Bristol, not London): MedTech company with BioEP, a patented digital biomarker platform for epilepsy diagnosis. £3m round co-led by Empirical Ventures and The FSE Group; also British Business Bank South West Investment Fund, Ascension Ventures, QantX, New Wave Venture Partners, and Angel Investors Bristol, plus a £400k Innovate UK Investor Partnerships grant. Previous funding: pre-seed (date unclear) then seed of c.£2m (April 2024). The 2026 round carries no stage label in the UKTN or FSE Group press releases; one secondary source calls it a "growth round." Flagged for human review on stage.
